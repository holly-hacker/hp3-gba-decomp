# Player Stats

This page shows the level-up table for the 3 playable characters. Note that this does not include an attack stat, which
is calculated based on level and [spell stats](./spells.html).

In general:

- `Exp.`, `Acc.`, `Def%` and `MDef%` are the same between characters
- Harry: low SP, low MP, medium speed
- Hermione: low SP, high MP, best speed
- Ron: high SP, low MP, worst speed

Lvl
: The player's stats at this level

SP
: The player's maximum stamina points

MP
: The player's maxiumum magic points, used

Exp.
: The _additional_ experience required to reach the next level.

Speed
: The inverse of the player's speed. Higher means later turn order.

Acc.
: The player's default accuracy, where >= 100 means it should always hit.

Def%
: The likelihood to dodge a standard attack

MDef%
: The likelyhood to dodge a special attack
