# Introduction

> [!IMPORTANT]
> If you are in any way (considering) contributing to The Cutting Room Floor, turn back now.
> 
> TCRF has a very clear policy on AI: If AI was used at any point during your reverse engineering process, you may not
> contribute to their wiki. This seems to include *any* kind of AI usage, which means all information in this book and
> the associated repo is effectively poisoned. Their wiki page on HP3-GBA containing calls for contribution, missing
> information or straight-up errors does not change this.

This book holds some information on Harry Potter and The Prisoner of Azkaban for the GameBoy Advance. Most of it was
derived through decompilation or dynamic analysis. It is my hope that this serves as an interesting "Wiki" of sorts for
ROM-hacking, speedrunning or just general playthroughs.

You can find a work-in-progress decompilation in [the associated repo](https://github.com/holly-hacker/hp3-gba-decomp).
With some tinkering, you can create basic ROM-hacks as well, including translations, assets swaps or general code
modifications.
